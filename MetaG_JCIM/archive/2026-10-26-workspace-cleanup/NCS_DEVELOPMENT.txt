User direction
--------------
Develop reliable chemical transfer, physical component validation, adaptive
calculation fidelity and metabolic utility for a Nature Computational Science
submission. Focus on published external measurements and computational
validation; prospective experimental collaborators are not available now.
Keep all existing work and all failures visible. Do not fit reaction offsets,
invent state constants, remove chemical-domain quarantines or market lower
accepted coverage as an accuracy improvement.

Ordered work and evidence gates
------------------------------
1. Preservation and evaluation integrity (implemented first).
   Protect source/artifact hashes. Audit reversed/scaled/stereo-equivalent
   reaction exposure, formation-label exposure, family exposure and Hess-law
   training-span membership. Retain reference eligibility, failed estimates,
   condition mismatches and accepted-coverage denominators in all comparisons.
   Distinguish family-held-out interpolation from compound-held-out transfer.
   This infrastructure alone is not an accuracy/generalization result.

2. Resolve the existing state-solvation mechanism (numerical checks incomplete).
   Continue the exact-geometry indolepyruvate keto/Z-enol study, not another
   broad severe-error sweep. CPU gas agreement and complete solvent agreement
   are separate checks. Preserve explicit overlap cutoffs, converged SCFs,
   solvent CDS, charge/spin, grids, geometries and source hashes.
   If robust, extend DFT/SMD to all ten saved anion minima using existing
   thermal terms. No fresh sampling is needed for that discriminating test.
   Do not enable global SMD: the existing balanced redox exchange barely moves,
   and selected-conformer solvent sensitivity is not experimental validation.

3. Build a condition-specific published state/reaction evidence ledger.
   Starter registry: analysis/ncs_development/external_state_references.json.
   Lopalco hydration data and Yang indolepyruvate data have prior development
   exposure; use them as component diagnostics, not blind external tests.
   Recover primary pyruvate tautomer-equilibrium text and conditions before
   numerical admission. Add independent sources with defined equilibria,
   species states, activities, temperature, solvent and original uncertainties.
   Do not transform temperature without enthalpy evidence, infer final pH from
   buffer preparation, or use total Mg concentration as free Mg activity.

4. Integrate state resolution and adaptive fidelity only after component checks.
   acid_hydration_cycle supplies a four-state reference model and closure audit;
   it is not automatically routed and supplies no guessed constants.
   Validate reaction-center and cofactor-core approximations independently;
   R versus R+1 agreement alone does not establish full-molecule accuracy.
   Establish coherent species/state reference potentials and reservoir terms.
   Compare full/core ladders and solvent treatments on fixed component panels.
   Require convergence evidence to select fidelity; experimental errors cannot
   choose the production route. Preserve budget/no-estimate outcomes.

5. Freeze and compare chemical transfer under common protocols.
   Include published dGbyG, a stereo-aware graph baseline, modern component
   contribution and a cheaper geometry method. Use common training data,
   identical holdout definitions and separately documented condition bridges.
   Compound holdouts remove relevant formation and reaction labels.
   Separate released-model external tests from newly trained holdout tests.
   Define chemical novelty and selection policies before inspecting errors.
   Report errors, severe tails, accepted coverage, cost, group-aware uncertainty
   and correlation of shared species errors. No universal transfer claim from
   TECRDB alone, an encoder collision or a lack of error/novelty correlation.

6. Demonstrate a consequential ModelSEED application and prepare release.
   Select a fixed pathway/substrate series with independently supported states
   and equilibrium/direction evidence. Test concentration-bounded feasibility
   and whether thermodynamics changes a meaningful design/curation decision.
   Integrate uncertainty coherently through stoichiometry; do not impose
   independent reaction perturbations or mistake cycle projection for accuracy.
   Release calculation specifications, accepted and failed results, geometries,
   state identities, source provenance, environment/model versions and costs.
   Manuscript claims and figures must follow completed evidence gates.

First implementation
--------------------
src/metag/validation/transfer.py: portable exposure and training-span audit.
src/metag/validation/evaluation.py: fixed-panel metrics and selective curves.
src/metag/tools/audit_transfer.py and evaluate.py: CPU commands with hashed
  inputs and refusal to overwrite existing evidence.
src/metag/validation/components.py: matched-coordinate numerical checks that
  distinguish completed gas comparisons from incomplete solvent calculations.
src/metag/energetics/state_cycles.py: conditional acid/hydrate populations,
  implied pKa and cycle diagnostics, without refitting measured constants.
src/metag/assembly.py: standalone S.T @ species_covariance @ S propagation;
  callers supply a justified covariance. Deployed reaction intervals unchanged.

New evidence lives under analysis/ncs_development; old artifacts stay intact.
See VALIDATION.txt there for exact check results and remaining limitations.
